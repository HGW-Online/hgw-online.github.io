# HGW — Asset & Resources Marketplace / Payment-Method Policy (proposed v0.1)

## Two wings, one cooperative commercial agency
1. Wing One: licensed partner second-hand listings, reuse and recycling; optional independent inspection, international shipping, customs brokerage, insurance and delivery.
2. Wing Two: assets, raw materials, resources, commodities, energy, real estate, machinery, production goods and service exchanges. Supports only **non-binding expressions of interest** for buy, sell, barter and collateral/mortgage until a properly licensed regulated workflow exists.

HGW is not automatically a counterparty, market maker, licensed exchange, bank, lender, custodian, broker/dealer, insurer or escrow service; HGW does not automatically take title to property or finance inventory.

## Proposed payment/settlement preference catalog (both wings)
- Cash, cheques, bank accounts, bank transfers, cards (Visa/Mastercard), regulated payment gateways, local/regional/global wallets, e-money, fiat currencies.
- Digital assets, cryptoassets, tokenized assets and stablecoins **only where authorized, with proper custody/settlement controls**.
- Barter, products, services, valuable goods, gold, precious metals, real estate, productive assets.
- Equities, shares, bonds, sukuk and other securities/receivables require securities, ownership, transfer and custody checks; *not a standard payment option by default*. Security collateral needs valid enforceable documents and valuations.
- Real estate mortgages, pledged inventories and other encumbrances require verified legal title, independent appraisal, an approved lender/collateral agent, valid registration/perfection where applicable, and appropriate enforcement/dispute procedures.
- "Anything with value" is **not** sufficient: valuation, transferability, liquidity, compliance and clear beneficial ownership must all be verified.

## Buyer freedom and service selection
Inspection, international freight, customs handling via a service provider, last-mile delivery, insurance and documentation are offered as independent optional service quotes unless a jurisdiction's law requires a particular service or document. Buyer chooses vendor; no mandatory bundling disguised as law.

## Marketplace and money safety
- No sensitive payment details, CVVs, card numbers, banking credentials, secret keys or wallet recovery phrases in listings, email or browser localStorage.
- Do not send currency, securities or tokens in response to an informal quote. No real asset or currency transfer through GitHub Pages.
- Before payments: connect licensed PSP/acquirer and regulated banking/escrow partners, KYB/KYC and AML/sanctions checks, consumer protection, fraud monitoring, refunds, dispute handling and contracts.
- Before securities trading: legal classification, market/venue/broker licenses and custody/settlement obligations in each jurisdiction.
- Before commodity/energy/food trading: product standards, food safety, permits, restricted exports, physical delivery rules and market abuse/derivatives classification where relevant.
- Before real estate: title registry, property law, notary/conveyancing, beneficial ownership and required tax/recordation.
- For cross-border: import/export eligibility, customs tariff and VAT assessment, shipping feasibility, insurance, sanctions and currencies.

## Finance & cooperative governance
Platform income belongs only to HGW under agreed contractual terms (exclude gross third-party sales/pass-through settlements). Apply mandatory expenses, taxes, returns and reserves first, then distribute **legally distributable surplus** between contribution-based special incentives and broad performance-based general incentives under approved audited bylaws. Direct and liquid democratic stakeholder governance is a design goal, **not live voting today**.

## Feature status
The pages currently provide category browsing, local-only demo listings, settlement-preference selection and a non-binding proposal summary. This is **not** a working banking, exchange, credit, collateral registration, payments or cooperative voting service.

## Minimal production milestones
1. Determine legal entity/jurisdictions and activity classification per asset and payment type.
2. Obtain licenses or partner with regulated custodians, payment processors and intermediaries.
3. Implement verified identity, ownership, beneficial ownership, legally binding agency contracts and escrow/e-money workflows.
4. Build central database with access controls, abuse prevention, auditable consent and order state machine.
5. Validate legal title, financial instruments, eligible collateral and quote validity; independent appraisals.
6. Test limited permitted transactions under human oversight before any live roll-out.

## Marketplace role and conditional success commission
HGW facilitates counterparties; it is not the owner, guarantor, bank, insurer, lender or custodian. Brokerage commissions apply only to contractually defined successful and verified transactions, with refund/dispute rules. Fees owed to independent optional service providers remain separate. The target international nonprofit-owned holding company and local branches are aspirational until incorporation and licensing. See [Global coordinator charter](GLOBAL-COORDINATOR-CHARTER.md).
