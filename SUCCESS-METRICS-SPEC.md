# HGW success-first performance scorecard — proposal v0.1

## Primary outcome (replaces vanity metrics)
Business success is assessed through independently verifiable **successfully concluded transactions**, **barriers actually removed**, **quality and efficiency of services**, **low total cost**, **speed**, **transparency**, and **friendly, fair and equal treatment of all customers**. Number of registered sectors, pages, offers, regions, or announcements is NOT a primary success metric.

## Required metrics
| KPI | Numerator / metric | Denominator / baseline | Auditable evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completed successful transactions | Confirmed closed sales/exchanges with paid settlement and required delivery/obligation confirmation, adjusted for returns and reversals | Started qualified transaction workflows | Transaction ledger, signed handover, reconciliation and disputes |
| Removed barriers | Unique obstacles closed with demonstrated practical resolution | Unique validated obstacles logged | Issue log with category, blocker owner, resolution proof, reopen events and dated review |
| Quality | Services meeting objective acceptance tests on first completion | Completed services assessed | Independent inspection and customer acceptance, incident reports |
| Efficiency | Completed service output per resources/hours, separately by activity | Comparable verified labor/time/cost input | Time- and cost-stamped operational records |
| Low total cost | (Comparable baseline landed cost - actual landed cost) / baseline landed cost | Same scope, taxes, quality and currency time | Buyer-facing signed quotes, market alternatives and actual invoices |
| Speed | Median hours from accepted request to agreed outcome, with p90 | Stage-specific baseline and same service category | Event timestamps, pauses and customer-caused delays disclosed |
| Transparency | Completed pre-contract disclosure of full costs, HGW success fee, conditions and responsibility | All qualified started transactions | Disclosure version, time and consent logs |
| Friendly customer care | Positive kindness/respect evaluation, with response rate and sample size | Eligible post-service survey responses | Aggregated post-service survey with methodology |
| Equal treatment | Accessibility and outcomes across comparable situations, complaint resolution and independent fairness audit | Comparable customer service groups and objective eligibility rules | Anonymous aggregate audit, accessible complaints and remedies |

## Protections
- Do not count spam or duplicate requests, reopened blockers as resolved, or refunded transactions as durable success.
- Never pressure customers to give positive ratings or penalize staff for reporting real problems.
- Measure savings only with equivalent scope, regulations, taxes, risk and service quality; never claim arbitrary price reduction.
- Do not use customer sensitive attributes as public KPIs; independently assess disparate impacts while following privacy law.
- Present sample size, missing data, baselines and confidence/limitations alongside metrics.
- Incentive calculation should reward balanced customer and service outcomes rather than one metric, with human appeals and independent audit.
- HGW is a coordinator paid success-only HGW commissions on verified contractual success; separately contracted independent vendor service fees may still arise for services rendered.
- Until connected to production event logs, any scorecard is a **demo**, not a public performance claim.

## Early pilot acceptance targets (proposed, not actual results)
- 5–10 verified successful legally permitted cross-border trades in a pilot, or evaluate why fewer completed.
- Trace each material obstacle from discovery to resolution or acknowledged block.
- Quote cost completeness and completion-time baseline for each selected pilot category.
- Document complete disclosure and customer consent in every test transaction.
- Publish honest sample sizes, customer feedback results and remediation actions.
